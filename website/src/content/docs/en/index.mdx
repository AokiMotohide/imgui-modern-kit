## How it fits

You call ImKit from inside your own Dear ImGui context. Ordinary controls read and write your values. Editor surfaces such as the Node Editor receive borrowed data and return requests. Your application manages the theme, saving, undo, and the context and backend.

<div class="home-points">
  <div><span class="point-index">01</span><strong>Pick the parts you need</strong><p>Link only the modules you use: controls, workflow, charts, the Editor Suite, the Node Editor or Show.</p></div>
  <div><span class="point-index">02</span><strong>Your application keeps the data</strong><p>Controls read and write your values. Editor surfaces exchange borrowed data and bounded edit requests with your code.</p></div>
  <div><span class="point-index">03</span><strong>Mix it with plain Dear ImGui</strong><p>ImKit components are drawn inside your frame, next to ordinary Dear ImGui calls.</p></div>

## Acknowledgements

ImKit is built on the public API of [Dear ImGui](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui). Thank you to Omar Cornut, and to every contributor and sponsor who builds and maintains it. ImKit does not modify Dear ImGui and does not replace it.

<p class="home-footnote">ImKit targets C++20 and a pinned revision of the Dear ImGui docking branch. Supported platforms and the ABI conditions are in <a href="./getting-started/">Getting started</a>.</p>
